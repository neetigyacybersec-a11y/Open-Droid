## What I do
- Write action-oriented labels in sentence case.
- Craft error messages: what happened + why + how to fix.
- Design empty states with explanation + next action.
- Keep onboarding minimal; avoid jargon.

## When to use me
Use when writing or reviewing any UI text.

## Anti-patterns
- Blaming users; vague errors; jargon; text in images.
